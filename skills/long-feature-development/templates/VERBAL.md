## complementary

A task may list optional attachments under its dedicated gists field, labelled reference only in previews. They must not appear in inputs, relationships, evidence, decision reasons or completion criteria. Deleting attachments or this index must not affect authoritative validation or resumption.

Generate the attachment list by scanning actual files, not by editing rows; dated reference notes may be added here.
