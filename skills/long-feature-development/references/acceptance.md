# User-facing acceptance

Read when preparing, presenting or recording an acceptance decision. Acceptance is a task type or task section; keep its brief and decision in a durable task, not in gists/ and not in a new top-level acceptance module.

## Prepare a short decision brief

Identify the designated decider and effective authority, then explain in plain language:

- the business outcome and scope being accepted, including exclusions;
- the exact candidate and the applicable requirement/solution versions;
- what changed and how to examine the result;
- criteria, checks actually performed, evidence and known limits;
- remaining risks or requested follow-up;
- the specific decision being requested and what that decision permits.

Link durable supporting tasks/design points. Internal IDs and raw logs are optional trace context, not knowledge the user must acquire before deciding. This reference specifies brief/response content, not when or how to proactively contact the user; that belongs to the external orchestrator's working methods. If a user decision is configured as a blocking prerequisite, use the [GATE communication contract](gates.md), not a separate interaction workflow imposed by acceptance type.

## Capture the actual response

A decider may accept, reject, request changes or defer pending a specified check. Natural language is valid if its target and meaning are clear. Preserve the source, scope, candidate, conclusion, actual decider, time and reason; clarify ambiguous replies without inventing approval.

The decider may be human or an authorized delegate according to the external mandate. Preparing the brief does not confer decision authority. Keep a pending or deferred decision visibly unresolved; distinguish task execution state from the acceptance conclusion.

Complete the acceptance task only when its assigned completion conditions and durable recording are met. Rejection may complete a decision-processing assignment; deferral alone does not satisfy an assignment that requires a final decision. Follow-up work remains explicit.

## Carry the decision forward accurately

Acceptance applies to the identified candidate and scope, not all future versions or release actions. A changed candidate needs an applicability assessment and any renewed acceptance required by external policy. Retain the old brief and decision.

If a GATE depends on acceptance, link its actual decision and evidence; task DONE alone is not a positive acceptance. Neither the brief nor the decision grants an unrequested merge or deployment.
